Sustainability Strategy

Cradle to Cradle Certification — Sustainable Product Strategy

MARK230

A strategic proposal for achieving Cradle to Cradle Gold certification on a designer reusable water bottle. I worked through all five certification categories: Material Health, Product Circularity, Clean Air and Climate Protection, Water and Soil Stewardship, and Social Fairness. I used Dopper as the reference point and built the proposal around what would actually be required to hit that standard.
