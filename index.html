About Me.

Hi, I’m Mark. I’m in my final year of a Business degree at the University of Wollongong, and I’ve spent the last few years throwing myself into as many different things as I can, studying, coaching, leading, competing, and working.

I like being across a lot at once. Whether it’s running a PASS session, building a campaign strategy, or competing in a pickleball tournament on a Saturday morning, I bring the same energy to all of it.

Most of what I’ve learned hasn’t come from a textbook. It’s come from coaching kids at tennis, working an events internship, facilitating study sessions, and figuring things out as I go. Academically I’ve finished in the top two students in four subjects and hold a WAM of 89.50, but I think what sets me apart is how I apply what I learn outside the classroom.

I’m open to where my career takes me. I care about doing work that’s creative, purposeful, and actually makes a difference to the people around me.

My Professional Focus.

I'm drawn to work that sits at the intersection of people, ideas, and execution. I like building things, whether that's a campaign, an event, or a session plan, and I like the moment when a plan actually comes together.
